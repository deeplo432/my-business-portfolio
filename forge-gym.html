01Philosophy
No mirrors on the wall — just chalk, iron, and a clock.

FORGE was built for people who want to get strong without the theatrics. One open floor, barbells, plates, and a program that changes every day so your body never gets comfortable.

We coach in person because form matters more than motivation. And when you're training on your own, our AI coach is there to answer the question a human isn't awake for.
