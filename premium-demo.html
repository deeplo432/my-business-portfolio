Our Story

Grown Here.
Cooked With Care.

The Copper Table opened in 2018 with one simple idea: cook honest food with ingredients we can trace to a specific farm, field, or family. We've been doing it ever since — changing the menu as Alabama's seasons do, and building relationships with the small growers who make it possible.

Chef Maria Calloway grew up in Blount County. Her food remembers that — rooted in Southern tradition, with the refinement of fifteen years cooking in Charleston and Nashville before coming home.
